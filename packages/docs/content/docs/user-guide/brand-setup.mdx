## Your First Brand

After signing in for the first time, BeatGeo shows the **Brand Onboarding** screen. This is where you tell BeatGeo about your brand.

{/* TODO: Screenshot of the brand onboarding screen */}

Enter your **website URL** (e.g. `https://yourbrand.com`) and click **Continue**. BeatGeo creates your brand profile and redirects you to the dashboard.

## What BeatGeo Extracts

From your website URL, BeatGeo identifies:

- **Brand name** — Parsed from your site's metadata
- **Primary domain** — The domain you entered
- **Industry context** — Used to generate relevant prompts in the next step

## Managing Brand Details Later

After initial setup, you can update your brand's details in **Settings → Brand**:

- **Name** — Your brand name as it should appear in AI responses
- **Website** — Your primary website URL
- **Domains** — Additional domains associated with your brand
- **Aliases** — Alternative names or spellings AI models might use
